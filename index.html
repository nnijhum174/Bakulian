Importance of Trees

Trees are very important for our life. They give us oxygen, fruits, flowers and wood. Trees keep the environment fresh and beautiful. They also provide shelter for birds and animals. We should plant more trees to protect our environment.

Benefits of Trees

  • Trees give us oxygen.
  • Trees provide fruits and flowers.
  • Trees give us shade.
  • Trees help to prevent soil erosion.
  • Trees keep the air clean.

Types of Trees

Tree Name Uses
Mango Tree Provides fruits
Neem Tree Provides medicine
Jackfruit Tree Provides fruits and wood
Teak Tree Provides wood

How to Protect Trees

  1. Plant more trees.
  2. Do not cut down trees unnecessarily.
  3. Take care of young plants.
  4. Spread awareness about tree plantation.
  5. Protect forests and wildlife.

Conclusion

Trees are our best friends. They are essential for a healthy life. So, we should plant more trees and save our environment.
